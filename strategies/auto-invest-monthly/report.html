How to Set Up Auto-Investing

1
Choose a Broker
M1 Finance — Best option. Create a "Pie" with 7 slices at the exact percentages above. Set recurring transfers on the 1st or 15th. Auto-invests automatically with fractional shares.

Fidelity — Use "Automatic Investments" for each position. Supports fractional shares on stocks and ETFs.

Schwab — "Stock Slices" for fractional shares; set up recurring buys manually per position.
Tip: M1 Finance is the most hands-off option for this exact strategy.
2
Set Up the Recurring Transfer
Link your bank account and schedule a recurring ACH transfer of $500–$1,000 on the 1st of each month. Starting on a fixed date builds discipline and removes emotion from the process.
DCA principle: Invest the same amount regardless of market conditions — you buy more shares when prices are low automatically.
3
Enable DRIP for MSFT and WMT
Enable dividend reinvestment (DRIP) for Microsoft and Walmart. Their dividends will automatically buy more fractional shares, compounding your ownership over time. MSFT yields ~0.75%; WMT ~1% — small now, significant in 10 years.
Over 20 years, DRIP can add 20–40% to total return vs. taking dividends as cash.
4
Manually Reinvest QQQI Distributions
QQQI pays monthly distributions (~13–16% annually). Do NOT enable DRIP for QQQI — instead, once a month, take those distributions and manually buy more MSFT or WMT. This redirects high-yield income into your strongest compounders.
At $750/mo into QQQI, expect ~$8–10/month in distributions to reinvest.
5
Quarterly Portfolio Review
Every quarter, run /portfolio-review with your current holdings to check for allocation drift. If any position is more than ±5% off target, use new monthly contributions (not selling) to rebalance. Monitor UNH specifically for DOJ/regulatory news.
Use the InvestSkill plugin's /portfolio-review skill for analysis.
6
Annual Strategy Review (Each January)
Once a year: reassess UNH (increase to 15% if regulatory overhang clears), review Bitcoin allocation (cap at 15% max), assess macro environment for gold/silver positioning. Consider adding new positions only if composite score ≥ 7.0.
Run /research-bundle on any new candidate before adding to the portfolio.

Risk Assessment — Portfolio Level

Low Risk — WMT + Gold (30%)

Walmart is recession-proof consumer staples. Gold is a safe-haven non-correlated to equities. Together they provide portfolio ballast in market downturns. Expected drawdown in a 30% market crash: 10–15%.

Moderate Risk — MSFT + QQQI + Silver (48%)

Microsoft has a wide moat and predictable revenues but carries tech sector correlation. QQQI has capped upside. Silver is more volatile than gold. Expected drawdown in a 30% market crash: 20–25%.

Higher Risk — Bitcoin + UNH (22%)

Bitcoin can fall 50–80% in bear markets. UNH faces regulatory overhang. Combined exposure is capped at 22% to limit portfolio damage. Even in a severe scenario, max damage to total portfolio is ~10–15%.

Concentration Risk

MSFT at 25% is the largest single holding. This is appropriate given its wide moat and high conviction, but should be reduced to 20% if it grows above 30% of total portfolio via appreciation.

UNH Specific Risk

DOJ investigation outcome is binary: resolution = significant upside. Adverse ruling or forced breakup = sustained downside. Monitor quarterly; reduce to 5% if thesis deteriorates further.

Inflation Scenario

23% in gold/silver + 12% Bitcoin = 35% hard-asset exposure. This portfolio is well-positioned for inflationary environments. Real assets protect purchasing power when paper assets erode.

Recession Scenario

WMT (defensive), Gold (safe haven), MSFT (cloud contracts are sticky) provide resilience. QQQI income generation continues. Only high-risk positions (IBIT, UNH) face significant pressure.

Maximum Drawdown Estimate

In a severe bear market (-40% equities), estimated portfolio drawdown: 25–30%. Recovery time at continued $750/mo DCA: 18–30 months. DCA accelerates recovery by buying more shares at lower prices.
